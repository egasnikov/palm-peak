Operating system

How we execute: five steps from an off-market floor to a stabilised, saleable income stream

1
Source below the market

Off-market floors from developers, banks and private owners, priced at AED 1,500-4,000/sqft against replacement cost and comparable sales of AED 1,900-5,700/sqft. Sky Tower at AED 1,522/sqft; Emaar Square at AED 3,176/sqft.

2
Convert in nine months

Contracted fit-out crews deliver a fully serviced floor (reception, suites, boardrooms, pantry, IT) at AED 300-550/sqft. Emaar Square: AED 8.0M on 26,447 sqft, works August to January.

3
Lease through the network

All-inclusive suites (DEWA, chiller, internet, cleaning) priced per sqft or per workstation, sold through 2,000+ brokers plus direct corporate outreach. Emaar Square: 10 units leased in four months.

4
Operate with data

Pricing, churn and renewal decisions run on live occupancy data; routine broker and tenant communication is ~80% automated. Result: higher rents, lower overhead, a cleaner rent roll at exit.

5
Sell stabilised income

A leased, serviced floor with multi-year covenants is sold on yield to an income buyer, or held for cash yield when the market pays for growth instead. Emaar Square: AED 150M offer at a 13% gross yield on our cost.

What the operating layer adds (Emaar Square Building 1, AED M per year)
