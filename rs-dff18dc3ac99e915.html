The Building, the Floor and the Target Product

Radiant Square is a five-tower mixed-use scheme by Radiant Enterprises (handover 2024-25) in the City of Lights precinct, 1 minute from Shams Boutik Mall and two blocks from Sky Tower. The office block occupies the lower floors; Rice Offices opens 14 suites on floors 5 and 10 in October 2026. The 6th floor sits between them on an identical plate.

Operating Build-Up (AED, lease years)

Lease year 1 carries the fit-out period (head rent, service charge and pre-opening payroll with no revenue) and the occupancy ramp. Leasing commissions of 10% of contracted annual rent fall in the trading years when sub-leases originate or renew (3-year sub-lease cycle). Core operating costs are built bottom-up for this floor in the table below and escalate 3% a year.

Method and assumptions. Monthly engine, 12,368 sq ft net lettable (1,149 sqm), the 6th floor of the Radiant Square office block as listed by Banke International in August 2026. Head lease at the AED 1.9M asking rent in year 1 (AED 1,653/sqm), 5% escalation, rent and service charge from day one, security deposit returned at term end. Fit-out AED 550/sq ft plus 5% contingency; 5% VAT on fit-out funded at closing and recovered in lease year 1. Set-up, legal and launch marketing AED 690,000. Capacity of 164 desks at 7.0 sqm each, matching the 165 desks Rice fits on the identical plate one floor below. Two months of pre-opening payroll. Trading opens at 60% of capacity desks, rising 5 points a month to stabilisation; 1% credit loss. Core operating costs AED 77,500 a month at 2026 prices, built bottom-up for this floor (payroll 42,000, utilities 15,600, pantry 5,300, maintenance 4,300, insurance and admin 3,300, internet and software 4,500, ongoing marketing 2,500; see the build-up table), 3% escalation; licence AED 50,000 a year. Leasing commission 10% of contracted annual rent on origination and each 3-year renewal. Operator: management fee on collected revenue, then the NOI split on each year's positive NOI (no loss carry-forward); the investor funds any negative year through a working-capital reserve sized at closing. Returns are pre-tax and unlevered; no residual value is assumed at lease expiry unless the optional business-sale control is switched on.
