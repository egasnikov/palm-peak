Operating Build-Up (AED, lease years)

Lease year 1 carries the fit-out period (head rent, service charge and pre-opening payroll with no revenue) and the occupancy ramp. Leasing commissions of 10% of contracted annual rent fall in the trading years when sub-leases originate or renew (3-year sub-lease cycle). Core operating costs (payroll, utilities, pantry, maintenance, insurance) are scaled by area from the Sky Tower reference budget and escalate 3% a year.

What the tenant pays if they do it themselves (AED / sqm / yr)

Conventional Grade A fitted space on Al Maryah: full-floor comps AED 4,350-4,400/sqm (Aug 2026 broker channel), prime headline above AED 6,000/sqm (Cushman & Wakefield H1 2026), fitted sublease listings from AED 2,500/sqm (Sep 2026). The DIY stack uses the mid-point and the same fit-out basis as this model, amortised over 5 years.

Method and assumptions. Monthly engine, 2,500 sqm gross (26,910 sq ft). Head lease at AED 7.5M in year 1 (AED 3,000/sqm), 5% escalation, rent and service charge from day one, security deposit returned at term end. Fit-out AED 550/sq ft plus 5% contingency; 5% VAT on fit-out funded at closing and recovered in lease year 1. Set-up, legal and launch marketing AED 690,000. Two months of pre-opening payroll. Trading opens at 60% of capacity desks, rising 5 points a month to stabilisation; 1% credit loss. Core operating costs AED 123,000 a month at 2026 prices (payroll, utilities, pantry, maintenance, insurance and admin, scaled by area from the Sky Tower Floor 38 budget), 3% escalation; licence AED 50,000 a year. Leasing commission 10% of contracted annual rent on origination and each 3-year renewal. Operator: management fee on collected revenue, then the NOI split on each year's positive NOI (no loss carry-forward); the investor funds any negative year through a working-capital reserve sized at closing. Returns are pre-tax and unlevered; no residual value is assumed at lease expiry unless the optional business-sale control is switched on.
